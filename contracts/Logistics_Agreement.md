## 1. Purpose and scope

Ownly generates personalised children's storybooks and, on customer request,
offers a printed physical copy of the digital story. This Agreement governs
the Partner's provision of one or both of:

- **(a) Print fulfilment**: producing a physical bound copy of a storybook
  from a digital file Ownly supplies, to the specification in Schedule 1; and/or
- **(b) Courier/delivery**: collecting completed orders and delivering them to
  the end customer's address within the UAE and, where agreed, internationally.

Where the Partner provides both services, both apply; where the Partner
provides only one (e.g. a courier who is not also the printer), only the
relevant clauses apply — mark which in Schedule 1.

## 2. Order flow

1. Ownly transmits an order to the Partner consisting of: the finished story
   PDF/print-ready file, the recipient's name, phone number, and delivery
   address, and an Ownly order reference. Ownly does **not** transmit the
   child's reference photo or any data beyond what Schedule 2 (Data Processing
   Agreement) permits.
2. The Partner confirms receipt and an estimated production/dispatch date
   within **[2] business days**.
3. The Partner prints and/or ships the order per Schedule 1's specification
   and provides Ownly a tracking reference (where courier services apply)
   within **[1] business day** of dispatch.
4. The Partner notifies Ownly promptly of any delay, defect, or delivery
   failure so Ownly can manage the customer relationship — the Partner has no
   direct customer-support obligation to Ownly's end customers under this
   Agreement unless Schedule 1 says otherwise.

## 3. Pricing and payment

Per-unit pricing is set out in Schedule 1. Ownly pays the Partner
**[monthly in arrears / per order, net 30 days]** against the Partner's
invoice, referencing Ownly's order reference(s). Prices are in **[AED/USD]**
and exclude VAT unless stated otherwise; VAT is added per UAE Federal Decree
-Law No. 8 of 2017 and its executive regulations where applicable.

## 4. Quality and specification

The Partner will produce/deliver each order to the specification in
Schedule 1. A defective, damaged-in-production, or materially
mis-specified copy will be reprinted or re-shipped by the Partner at its own
cost within **[5] business days** of Ownly reporting the defect, without
charge to Ownly or the end customer.

## 5. Data protection

The Partner's handling of any personal data received under this Agreement —
the end customer's name, phone number, and delivery address, and the finished
story file — is governed by **Schedule 2 (Data Processing Agreement /
DPA_Schedule.md)**, which is incorporated into and forms part of this
Agreement. In the event of any conflict between this Agreement and Schedule 2
on a data-protection matter, Schedule 2 prevails.

## 6. Confidentiality

Each Party will keep the other's confidential business information
(pricing, volumes, customer data, and this Agreement's terms) confidential,
using it only to perform this Agreement, for **[3] years** after termination.
This clause does not limit either Party's obligations under Schedule 2, which
survive independently per its own terms.

## 7. Liability

Each Party's liability to the other under this Agreement is limited to the
fees paid or payable under this Agreement in the **[12] months** preceding the
claim, except for: (a) breach of Schedule 2 (data protection), (b) breach of
confidentiality under clause 6, (c) death or personal injury caused by
negligence, or (d) fraud — none of which are subject to that cap. Neither
Party is liable for indirect or consequential loss.

## 8. Term and termination

This Agreement begins on the date above and continues until terminated by
either Party on **[30] days'** written notice. Either Party may terminate
immediately for the other's material, uncured breach (with **[14] days** to
cure after written notice) or insolvency. Schedule 2's data-protection
obligations survive termination for as long as the Partner holds any
Ownly-customer personal data, and the Partner must delete or return all such
data within **[30] days** of termination per Schedule 2.

## 9. Independent contractors

The Parties are independent contractors. Nothing in this Agreement creates a
partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship between them.

## 10. Governing law and disputes

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates as applied
in the Emirate of Dubai. The Parties will first attempt to resolve any
dispute by good-faith negotiation; failing resolution within **[30] days**,
the dispute will be referred to **[the courts of Dubai / DIFC-LCIA
arbitration — to be confirmed with counsel]**.

## 11. Notices

Notices under this Agreement must be in writing and sent to the addresses
above (or an updated address notified in writing), by email with confirmed
receipt or by courier.
